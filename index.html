Trail-Oriented Development in the Bay Area

Trail-oriented development (TrOD) is a planning framework that uses off-street trails and greenways as anchors for compact, context-sensitive development. It connects people to key destinations, nature, and each other through walkable, human-scaled design tailored to local conditions and priorities, while advancing equitable community development.

Marina Bay Bay Trail

The Marina Bay neighborhood in Richmond offers direct connections from homes to the SF Bay Trail.

Ohlone Greenway

The Ohlone Greenway in El Cerrito provides a last-mile connection to the Bay Area Rapid Transit (BART) Station

Emeryville Greenway

The Emeryville Greenway is a a 1.9-mile pedestrian and bicycle path that runs through through Emeryville from Berkeley to Oakland. This greenway connects people to employment, residences, shopping, regional transit, and the Bay Trail.
